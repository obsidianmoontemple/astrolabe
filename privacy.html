Privacy Policy

Temple of the Obsidian Moon app · Last updated: [DATE]

The short version. This app does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter — names, intentions, birth details, progress — stays on your own device. We have no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no advertising. We cannot see what you do in this app.

What stays on your device

Several parts of the Temple let you save things so they are still there when you come back. All of it is stored locally, in your device's own storage, and none of it is transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Names or nicknames you choose, including a child's chosen name and the name of their familiar in the Kid Zone
  • Progress through lessons, quests, and games
  • Candle intentions, altar offerings, and drawn sigils
  • Birth date, time, and place entered into the Astrolabe to calculate a chart
  • Coordinates you enter or allow the app to detect, used to draw the sky above you

Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, permanently erases all of this. We keep no copy and cannot restore it.

Children's privacy

The Kid Zone is designed to be used by children, and we have deliberately built it so that no information about a child is collected, transmitted, or stored anywhere other than on the family's own device. A child's chosen name, their familiar's name, and their progress never leave the phone or tablet they are using.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and no parental consent mechanism is required. There is no chat, no messaging, no way for a child to communicate with anyone through this app, and no way for anyone to contact a child through it.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about the Kid Zone, you are welcome to contact us at the address below.

Location

The Astrolabe can use your device's location to draw the stars as they appear above you. Granting this is entirely optional — you can type coordinates in by hand, or search for a city instead, and the rest of the app works normally without it. When you do allow it, your coordinates are used on your device to perform the calculation and are never sent to us.

When the app connects to the internet

The Temple is built to work offline. Two features reach out to the internet when you use them:

  • City search in the Astrolabe sends only the place name you typed to the Open-Meteo geocoding service, which returns coordinates and a time zone. Nothing about you is included.
  • Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts the first time the app runs, after which they are cached on your device.

These services will see the network request itself, including your device's IP address, as is normal for any internet connection. We do not receive that information.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising of any kind
  • We do not create accounts or profiles about you
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not hold any
  • We do not use your information to train anything

Purchases

The Temple of the Obsidian Moon is a paid app. Your purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Refunds are handled through Google Play under their standard terms.

Your rights

Because all of your information stays on your device, you already have complete control of it: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app and it is gone. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to return, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you would like to confirm this or ask anything about it, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that collects information, we will update this policy before that feature goes live and change the date at the top of this page. If the change affects children, we will obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting anything.
